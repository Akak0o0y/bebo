# Community guidelines

Bebo welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
Be patient, explain your reasoning, and help people reproduce and understand
problems. Critique ideas and code respectfully.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content directed at contributors,
spam, and publication of another person's private information are not welcome.
Do not post credentials, private recordings, or sensitive desktop screenshots.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions,
or restrict participation. Serious or repeated behavior can lead to a ban.
Use GitHub's **Report content** action to report abuse privately and include
the relevant permalink. Do not reproduce another person's private information
in a public report.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces. Technical disagreements are welcome when the discussion
remains specific, respectful, and focused on the work.
